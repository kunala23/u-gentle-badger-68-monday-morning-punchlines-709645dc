Entry for the [Monday Morning Punchlines](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc) bounty on make.fun: one joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters.

## The entry

> My bed filed for custody of me on Monday morning — and won, briefly.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, runs TypeScript as-is) scores three candidate jokes against the judge's five published criteria — really about Monday mornings, a real laugh with a turn, surprise, economy of words, freshness — and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, no secrets.

Run it locally:

```sh
node main.ts
```
